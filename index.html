Crafting

Time pauses while this is open. Campfires, the lean-to and the raft are placed in front of you after crafting.

How to survive

WASD or arrows: walk
E or Space: use / gather
C: crafting
F: eat the best food
Esc: cancel placing
M: sound on/off

Keep your four needs up. Hunger and thirst fall all day. Warmth falls after dark and in the rain, so stay close to a lit campfire at night. If any need hits zero you start losing health.

Drink at the freshwater pond. Click a food in your bag to eat it. Cooked fish and crab fill you up far better than raw.

Your goal: build a raft in the shallows, pack three cooked meals and sail home. Your progress saves automatically in this browser.
